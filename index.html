Mycobiome Analysis · Oulanka, Finland

Oulanka MycoPins Scientific Analysis Dashboard

A collection of reproducible analyses characterizing saproxylic fungal communities associated with deadwood in forest habitats of Oulanka, Finland. This research is conducted under the Shumskaya Lab at Kean University, with Dr. Maria Shumskaya serving as Principal Investigator. The dashboard serves as the main entry point to analytical reports generated from R-based Jupyter notebooks and rendered with Quarto.

Dashboard scope

Exploratory data analysis, within-community diversity, between-community variation, and taxa associated with specific ecological groups or conditions.

Study overview

The analyses focus on ecological patterns in a mycobiome dataset describing saproxylic fungi occurring on deadwood across forest sampling locations. The work is conducted under the Shumskaya Lab at Kean University.

Study system Saproxylic fungal communities
Location Oulanka, Finland
Primary environment Forest deadwood
Research laboratory Shumskaya Lab, Kean University
Principal Investigator Dr. Maria Shumskaya

Analysis 02

Alpha Diversity

Evaluates diversity within fungal assemblages using Hill-number diversity, rarefaction and extrapolation, and sample-coverage-based comparisons.

  • Species richness
  • Shannon and inverse Simpson diversity
  • Rarefaction, extrapolation, and coverage
